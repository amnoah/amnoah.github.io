Genesee County Medical History Project

Preserving our medical history

This project aims to preserve every major medical institution that has ever existed in Genesee County, Michigan. This includes the people, events, policies, and advancements that defined our past. Explore an interactive map and time chart to better understand this information.

Interactive Map

Interact with Genesee County's medical past through an interactive map. Toggle what is shown and play with a time slider to explore our past.
